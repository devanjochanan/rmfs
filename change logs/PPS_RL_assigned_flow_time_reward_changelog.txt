PPS RL assigned-order flow-time reward changelog
Date: 2026-05-18

Files changed:
- pps_env.py
- train_pps_rl.py

What changed:
- Replaced the PPS RL reward from average completion-time delta to assigned-order flow-time cost delta.
- The reward now uses the current completion-time definition: time starts when an order is assigned to a picker/workstation.
- Completed assigned orders contribute their final processing time.
- Unfinished assigned orders contribute their current age, so orders that remain waiting are still penalized.

New flow-time cost:
completed_orders_time_sum =
    sum(order_complete_time - process_start_time for completed assigned orders)

unfinished_orders_age_sum =
    sum(current_time - process_start_time for unfinished assigned orders)

total_flow_time_cost =
    completed_orders_time_sum + unfinished_orders_age_sum

New step reward:
reward_t =
    -reward_alpha * (total_flow_time_cost_t - total_flow_time_cost_t-1)

TensorBoard changes:
- pps_reward now logs only:
  total_reward
  total_flow_time_cost
  completed_orders_time_sum
- pile_on_rate remains under pps_metrics.
- picked_quantity and pod_visits are not logged under pps_reward.

CSV metrics changes:
- Added total_flow_time_cost.
- Added completed_orders_time_sum.
- Added unfinished_orders_age_sum.
- Added reward_flow_time_cost_delta.

Reason:
- The previous average completion-time delta only produced reward when orders completed.
- That made the reward sparse and allowed unfinished assigned orders to avoid direct time penalty until completion.
- The new flow-time cost keeps the reward measured in time while penalizing unfinished assigned orders as they wait.

Performance note:
- Flow-time cost metrics are now refreshed once at each PPS decision boundary instead of on every internal warehouse tick.
- This keeps the reward semantics the same while reducing repeated full-order scans during simulation advancement.
- Added RMFS_FAST_TRAIN mode for PPO training.
- When RMFS_FAST_TRAIN=1, reporting-only CSV/SQLite writes are skipped or kept in memory:
  job_task, order_history, pod_travel, pre_assign, pod_info.csv, and order-finished.csv.
- assign_order.csv and pod_location remain active in fast training because they are used by the simulation as state, not only as reporting.
- Order generation, pod-SKU randomization, POA/PPS logic, robot movement, and reward calculation remain active.
- Fixed a fast-training crash where a completed order with a missing station_id could raise KeyError: None during station removal.
- The finish handler now falls back to the pod job's station_id, and PPS job creation ensures picked orders carry the assigned station_id.

Important note:
- This reward does not include time before an order is assigned to a workstation.
- Throughput and unassigned/backlog behavior should still be monitored separately during evaluation.
